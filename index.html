  • All 12 teams still hold their full $200 (0 used), so competition is at its maximum and bids run high.

Capped at 45% of remaining budget (60% for a 5+ point gain). This is a judgment rule, not a calibrated model; the accuracy tracker will tell us over time whether the gains were real.
